About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in 1979.

UMT was established as a full university on 1 February 2007. It focuses on higher education, research and innovation, particularly in marine science, maritime studies and environmental fields.

Today, UMT provides various academic programmes and research opportunities. The university aims to develop knowledgeable graduates and contribute to society through education and research.
