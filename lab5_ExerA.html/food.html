About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is an exciting event that celebrates traditional food and local culture. Visitors can enjoy various delicious foods from Terengganu.

This festival brings together local food sellers and visitors to experience the unique taste of Terengganu. Everyone is welcome to join this special event.
